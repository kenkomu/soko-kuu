# Soko Kuu: 90-second judge path

Open the app (web link or APK). Works with no runner (offline demo) or against a live runner. Points are for play only; no cash-out.
Tip for screenshots: use a phone-width window (about 390 px) and capture after each numbered step.

| Time | Step | What to tap | What to see (screenshot this) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Age gate | Read the short rules, tap **I am 18 or older. Start** | Welcome card, "Karibu sokoni" |
| 0:10 | Guest play | Tap **Play as a guest (no email)** | Soko tab with today's prices. Banner: "Points are for play only. They can't be cashed out." |
| 0:20 | Language | Menu/settings: switch **English / Kiswahili** | Whole UI in Swahili |
| 0:30 | First sale | Follow the guided card: open a crop row, tap **Buy**, wait for midday if told, then **Sell** | Result shown at once; a "Why this price" explanation under the row |
| 0:50 | Duka | Open the **Duka** tab | Your stall, stock and points |
| 1:00 | Chama | Open **Chama**, tap **Join** on a chama (add an email only if asked), look at the lot on offer and the group vote | Pot, members, vote counts, plain explanation of the pooled price |
| 1:15 | Fast-forward | Open **Ripoti**, scroll to **What if? Fast-forward**, pick 30 days and **Drought**, tap **Run preview** | A preview of prices and money over 30 days. Your game is unchanged |
| 1:30 | Done | Optional: **Deni** tab for credit limits | Limits and repayment dates |

Notes for judges
- Offline demo uses a simplified chama (NPC members, fixed pot). Against the runner, chama loans and votes are real.
- Guests lose progress if app data is cleared (the app says so). Web keys are throwaway demo keys.
- Source of truth for prices is rule-based code; no AI writes prices.

Live runner: https://app.chukua.xyz (the app is built with `API_BASE=https://app.chukua.xyz`; without it the app runs the offline demo).
